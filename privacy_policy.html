Privacy Policy for Color Flood

Effective date: 29 August 2026

Last updated: 29 August 2026  ·  supersedes the version dated 15 July 2025

SoftMobiXBD ("we", "us") publishes the Color Flood mobile game (the "app"). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information. It applies to the Android app published under the package name com.softmobixbd.color_foold.

The short version. Color Flood has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information to our own servers — we do not operate any. Your progress lives only on your device. The one third party that receives data is Google AdMob, which serves the optional reward videos that fund the game.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. Color Flood requires no registration, no login, no email address, and no profile. We do not operate a server, an analytics pipeline, or a crash reporting service, so no information about you or your device reaches us.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your settings and progress locally using Android's SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device, is not backed up to us, and is not readable by other apps. It consists of:

  • Settings — theme, chosen colour palette, colourblind mode, reduce-motion, haptics, sound effects, music, last difficulty played, and whether you have finished the tutorial.
  • Progress — Journey stars and unlocked level, Daily Challenge streak, best streak and cleared dates, the current Zen board, wins and losses.
  • In-game currency — your coin balance and whether the "remove ads" entitlement is active.

None of this identifies you. You can erase all of it at any time from Settings → Reset all progress, and uninstalling the app removes it completely.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Color Flood is free. It is funded by optional reward videos — short ads you choose to watch in exchange for extra moves. Ads are served by Google AdMob.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • device and app information — device model, operating system version, language, and the app the ad was shown in;
  • ad interaction data — that an ad was shown, viewed, or clicked.

This processing is governed by Google's own privacy terms, not ours. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Color Flood does not display banner ads, and never interrupts a puzzle in progress with an ad.

Your choices about ads

  • You can simply not watch them. Reward videos are always optional — every board in the game is completable without one.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Choosing "Delete advertising ID" stops personalised advertising across all apps on the device.

4. Purchases

Any purchase offered inside Color Flood is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card, billing address, or Google account details. The app asks Google Play only whether an entitlement is owned, and records the yes/no answer locally so that it survives a reinstall.

5. What the app does not do

The app requests only internet access, which is used exclusively for loading ads and for Google Play Billing. Every mode of the game — Journey, Daily Challenge and Zen — is fully playable offline.

6. Children

Color Flood is not directed to children under 13 and is rated for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will act on it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. Data held on your device is under your control:

  • Settings → Reset all progress erases stars, streaks, statistics, and coins.
  • Uninstalling the app removes every stored value.
  • Advertising data held by Google is managed through your Google account and Android settings, as described in section 3.

8. Security

Game data is stored in the app's private storage area, which Android isolates from other apps. Network traffic to Google's advertising and billing services is encrypted in transit.

9. International use

Color Flood is available worldwide and this single policy applies in every region. Ads are delivered by Google, which may process data on servers outside your country under its own safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of the page changes with it, and the current version is always published at this address. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
